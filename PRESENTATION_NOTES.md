### Slide 3: Problem Statement: Traditional Domestic Help Challenges
* **Visual**: High-contrast comparison between traditional word-of-mouth hiring issues and Sparkle Home digital solutions.
* **Speaker Script**:
  > *"When we examine the traditional domestic labor market in India, we discover severe structural inefficiencies:*  
  > *1. **Trust and Safety Concerns**: Word-of-mouth references provide zero background checks, leaving homeowners worried about safety and theft.*  
  > *2. **Arbitrary Pricing**: Informal price negotiations lead to inconsistent rates with zero transparency.*  
  > *3. **Erratic Attendance & Sudden Leaves**: When maids take unannounced leave, families face immense stress with zero replacement backup.*  
  > *4. **Scheduling Conflicts**: Helpers frequently get double-booked across apartment complexes.*  
  > *5. **Economic Vulnerability for Workers**: Domestic helpers often face unfair wage cuts, informal exploitation, and middleman agency commissions.*  
  > *Sparkle Home replaces this informal, chaotic process with verified identities, standardized rate cards, locked calendar scheduling, and automated standby backups."*

### Slide 6: Tri-Role User Architecture & Ecosystem
* **Visual**: 3 distinct role columns: Customer, Maid/Worker, and Admin.
* **Speaker Script**:
  > *"The platform is architected around three core user roles:*  
  > *1. **Customer**: Can search helpers by locality, inspect verified badges and ratings, book one-time or monthly recurring plans, pay securely, and track service status with a 4-digit start OTP.*  
  > *2. **Domestic Helper / Maid**: Has a dedicated worker dashboard with a duty status toggle (Available vs. On Leave), receives incoming booking requests with instant Accept or Decline options, and views accumulated earnings with bank payout statuses.*  
  > *3. **Super Admin**: Exercises operational oversight via an admin center, reviewing pending worker KYC documents (Aadhaar & Police clearances), monitoring live bookings, resolving customer dispute tickets, and tracking financial metrics."*

### Slide 7: Major Platform Technical Features
* **Visual**: Grid highlighting Authentication, Verification, Geolocation, Scheduling, Pricing, Payments, and Reviews.
* **Speaker Script**:
  > *"Technically, the platform incorporates high-grade web engineering practices:*  
  > *• **Role-Based Authentication**: Secure customer, worker, and admin authentication using JWT and bcrypt hashing.*  
  > *• **KYC Verification Workflow**: Mandatory ID verification before a helper appears in customer search results.*  
  > *• **Proximity-Based Search**: Matches families with helpers residing within a 3-5 km radius for punctual arrivals.*  
  > *• **Conflict-Free Availability Calendar**: Automatically locks slots to prevent double-booking.*  
  > *• **Transparent Fare Engine**: Instantly computes base fare, 5% GST, and a nominal ₹29 platform safety fee.*  
  > *• **Multi-Payment Gateway**: Integrates Razorpay for instant UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit/debit cards, and Cash on Delivery.*  
  > *• **OTP Verification**: A 4-digit security OTP guarantees that only the assigned, background-checked helper enters the customer's residence."*

### Slide 9: End-to-End Booking & Service Workflow
* **Visual**: 6-step linear lifecycle from registration to review.
* **Speaker Script**:
  > *"The end-to-end user journey is designed for friction-free simplicity:*  
  > *Step 1: The customer registers and inputs their residential locality.*  
  > *Step 2: They select the desired domestic chore (e.g. Cooking + House Cleaning).*  
  > *Step 3: The system lists verified helpers nearby; the customer compares ratings, experience, languages, and rates.*  
  > *Step 4: The customer selects their preferred date and morning or evening slot.*  
  > *Step 5: Booking is confirmed with transparent pricing and payment via UPI or COD.*  
  > *Step 6: The helper arrives, verifies the 4-digit OTP, completes the service, and both parties exchange ratings."*

### Slide 10: Suggested Modern Technology Stack
* **Visual**: Layer-by-layer architectural stack diagram.
* **Speaker Script**:
  > *"Our proposed and implemented technology stack balances scalability, speed, and cross-device responsiveness:*  
  > *• **Frontend Layer**: React.js / Responsive semantic HTML5 with Tailwind CSS and Lucide Icons, providing a mobile-first design with bottom sticky navigation bars and touch drawers.*  
  > *• **Backend API**: Node.js with Express.js or Python Flask REST API handling authentication middleware, routing, and booking controllers.*  
  > *• **Database Layer**: MongoDB (Atlas) for flexible document schemas managing worker profiles, service catalogs, and live bookings.*  
  > *• **Location Engine**: Google Maps Geocoding and Distance Matrix API for hyperlocal worker matching.*  
  > *• **Payment Gateway**: Razorpay API for PCI-DSS compliant UPI, card processing, and escrow handling.*  
  > *• **Media Storage**: Cloudinary for worker profile pictures and encrypted KYC document uploads."*
