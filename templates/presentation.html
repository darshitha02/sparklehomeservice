1. Project Overview

Connecting Households with Verified Domestic Helpers

Digital Domestic Marketplace

Sparkle Home is a responsive, web-based and mobile-friendly platform connecting customers with verified domestic workers such as maids, house cleaners, cooks, babysitters, and senior companions.

  • • Browse available helpers by locality and rating
  • • Inspect transparent pricing without middleman bargaining
  • • Select flexible schedules: hourly, daily, or monthly

Mission & Social Impact

Aims to make hiring domestic helpers easier, safer, more transparent, and more reliable while providing steady, dignified employment opportunities for domestic workers across India.

100% Verified Aadhaar & Police Vetted
< 60 Mins Emergency Dispatch
2. Problem Statement

Traditional Domestic Help Challenges in India

Traditional Word-of-Mouth Issues

  • ✕ Trust & Security: No identity verification or background checks.
  • ✕ No Comparison: No platform to compare skills, ratings, or prices.
  • ✕ Arbitrary Pricing: Informal haggling with zero rate standardization.
  • ✕ Scheduling Chaos: Double bookings and unpredictable arrival.
  • ✕ Zero Accountability: Sudden unannounced leaves with no replacement.

Sparkle Home Digital Resolution

  • ✓ 100% Police Verified: Government ID authentication for all workers.
  • ✓ Transparent Rates: Clear hourly (₹149-₹299/hr) and monthly plans.
  • ✓ Guaranteed Replacement: Automated standby backup if helper is sick.
  • ✓ Locked Calendars: Real-time slot locking eliminates double booking.
  • ✓ Worker Opportunities: Direct access to households with direct bank pay.

5. User Roles Architecture

Tri-Role Operating Model

A. Customer / User

Search helpers by city, select services, book date/time slots, pay via UPI/Cash, track booking with OTP, and review.

B. Maid / Service Provider

Register profile, upload photo & Aadhaar KYC, set custom rates, manage duty toggle (Active/Leave), accept/reject jobs, and track weekly earnings.

C. Super Admin

Central operations desk, verify worker KYC & police clearances, monitor all live bookings, manage disputes, and oversee financial analytics.

12. Expected Project Outcome

The "Urban Company" for Domestic Help

Transforming the Domestic Sector

Sparkle Home establishes a reliable digital marketplace for domestic services, eliminating the hassle, insecurity, and friction of finding and booking domestic helpers.

Similar to how Urban Company digitized home repairs and beauty, Sparkle Home formalizes the daily maid, cook, and childcare ecosystem with special focus on Indian households.

Mutual Win-Win Ecosystem

For Households: Verified safety, predictable pricing, zero sudden leave anxiety, instant emergency dispatch.
For Domestic Workers: Fair standardized wages, direct digital bank payments, schedule autonomy, dignity of labor.
